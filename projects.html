Derivatives

Options Portfolio VaR: Delta-Normal vs. Delta-Gamma vs. Monte Carlo

A book of SPX options (long straddle, short puts, call spread) priced with Black-Scholes, using VIX as implied vol. Compares the three VaR methods, shows how delta-normal underestimates risk on non-linear positions, and simulates the underlying with GARCH instead of constant volatility.
