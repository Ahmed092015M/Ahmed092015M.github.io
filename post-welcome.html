Why I'm starting this blog

I've spent a good part of my career building models that end up in a spreadsheet, a report or a risk system, and very little of that work is visible from the outside. This blog is my way of changing that.

I'll use it to write up the projects I'm publishing on GitHub, and the things I learn while building them. Expect posts on:

  • Market and portfolio risk: VaR and Expected Shortfall, and how to backtest them properly.
  • Portfolio construction: why the covariance matrix matters more than the optimizer, and what cleaning it actually changes out of sample.
  • Forecasting: power prices and load, and the gap between a model that fits well and one that forecasts well.
  • Systematic strategies: what survives walk-forward testing and realistic transaction costs.

The posts will be practical, with code and results, and honest about where a model breaks.

If you work on similar problems, I'd be glad to hear from you on LinkedIn.
