Hi, I'm Ahmed Baahmed

Quantitative analyst working on portfolio risk, quantitative research and forecasting.

I live in Luxembourg and have been building statistical and quantitative models for more than 12 years, first for financial institutions and energy companies, then in data science and quantitative finance. With a post-graduation in applied mathematics: a postgraduate degree in Probability, Stochastic Analysis and Finance at the University of Luxembourg, and an M.Sc. in Operations Research, Optimization and Decision Making.

What I enjoy most is taking a model from an idea to something a team can actually use to make decisions, whether that's a VaR model, a trading signal or a price forecast.

What I work on

Market & portfolio risk

GARCH-based VaR and Expected Shortfall, stress tests, and backtesting with Kupiec and Christoffersen tests.

Quantitative research

Researching and backtesting systematic strategies such as statistical arbitrage, mean reversion and factor models, using walk-forward tests and realistic transaction costs.

Portfolio construction

Mean-variance, Hierarchical Risk Parity, robust optimization, risk budgeting, and cleaning noisy covariance matrices with shrinkage and random matrix theory.

Machine learning

Gradient boosting for forecasting, and LSTM and autoencoder models for time-series anomaly detection, always compared with simple statistical baselines.

Energy markets

Day-ahead power price and load forecasting, and regime-switching models for price spikes.

Model validation

Checking how models behave out of sample, and writing down what they assume and where they break.
